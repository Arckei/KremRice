KremRice started as a small student project sa PUP — a love letter to the kakanin our lolas used to sell along the street, given a playful modern twist. We work with local rice farmers, cook everything by hand in small batches, and hide a little surprise inside every ball. Because "sarap" is good — but "sarap na may surprise" is better.

No shortcuts, no preservatives — just bigas, gata, and a lot of patience.
